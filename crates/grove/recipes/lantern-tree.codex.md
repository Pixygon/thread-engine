# Lantern Tree

The crystal-like tree of the [[lantern-desert]], at the east end of [[caul]].
It bears **large fruits resembling the lantern of a deep-sea fish**, glowing
bright blue with hints of orange, hung at the ends of its branches. The wood
itself is crystalline — pale, veined, faintly lit — and its tips reach upward
rather than droop.

## After [[the-aetherfall]]

The trees **wither**: the light goes out of the wood, which darkens and
gnarls, and the fruits **drop to the ground**, where each leaves a large
puddle of lava-like liquid, shining blue with streaks of orange. A withered
lantern tree is not a different tree — it is the same one, on the other side
of [[the-two-eras]].
